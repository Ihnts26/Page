Statement Concerning Information, Monitoring, Privacy, Disclosure, and Misrepresentation

I hereby place on record my objections regarding circumstances in which my attention may be deliberately drawn to particular persons, events, activities, photographs, communications, or other circumstances, while persons involved may subsequently deny that I was the subject of their attention or conduct.

I further object to any undisclosed observation, monitoring, surveillance, investigation, information-gathering, or collection of information concerning me, particularly where information obtained through such means may subsequently be communicated to other persons or used to construct, support, or promote assertions concerning my identity, activities, relationships, intentions, or conduct.

Information, Monitoring, and Observation

I hereby place on record my objections regarding circumstances in which my attention may be deliberately drawn to particular persons, events, activities, photographs, communications, or other circumstances, while persons involved may subsequently deny that I was the subject of their attention or conduct.

I further object to any undisclosed observation, monitoring, surveillance, investigation, information-gathering, or collection of information concerning me, particularly where information obtained through such means may subsequently be communicated to other persons or used to construct, support, or promote assertions concerning my identity, activities, relationships, intentions, or conduct.

If any such conduct has occurred, the source, purpose, date, circumstances, method of acquisition, scope, accuracy, and subsequent use, retention, or disclosure of information concerning me should be accurately identified and preserved.

Personal Knowledge and Direct Relationships

I am particularly concerned about circumstances in which persons who have never personally interacted with me, communicated with me, met me, or otherwise established a direct relationship with me may nevertheless represent or communicate to others that they "know" me or know matters concerning me.

A person's assertion that they know me should not be treated as established fact merely because they have received information about me from another source, observed me indirectly, obtained information through undisclosed means, or formed an interpretation from unrelated circumstances.

Information obtained about a person is not the same as personal knowledge of that person. Secondhand information, indirect observations, photographs, social-media material, employment history, statements from third parties, proximity, coincidence, or other circumstantial information should not be presented as evidence of personal familiarity, a personal relationship, or direct interaction where none has occurred.

Attention, Awareness, and Alleged Involvement

The fact that my attention has been drawn to another person or circumstance, or that I have noticed, reacted to, or become aware of something, does not establish that I am involved in that person's affairs, communicating with them, following them, monitoring them, imitating them, or otherwise participating in matters concerning them.

Likewise, similarities in some circumstances do not by themselves establish that I am intentionally imitating, following, monitoring, communicating with, or attempting to resemble another person.

Selective Combination and Construction of Narratives

I object to any attempt to selectively combine observations, photographs, social-media material, employment information, communications, possessions, statements made by other persons, or other information to construct a narrative that I am involved in another person's affairs or intentionally imitating another person.

This objection applies particularly where the circumstances through which the information was obtained are omitted, minimized, inaccurately described, or disconnected from their original context.

Information should not be selectively arranged or presented in a manner that creates a misleading impression of a relationship, intention, pattern, or course of conduct.

Disclosure and Communication of Personal Information

I further object to the disclosure, circulation, or discussion of personal information concerning me with unrelated persons where there is no legitimate and lawful reason for doing so.

This objection applies particularly where persons receiving or discussing such information have never personally interacted with me, communicated with me, met me, or otherwise established a direct relationship with me.

This objection also applies to the disclosure or communication of information about me to politicians, public officials, political figures, organizations, or other persons who have not personally interacted with me.

The fact that information may have been communicated to such persons does not establish that those persons personally know me, have direct knowledge of me, or have a direct relationship with me.

Any distinction between direct knowledge, information received from others, and assumptions or interpretations should be maintained.

Privacy of Personal Experiences and Circumstances

Some personal experiences, circumstances, interactions, events, communications, employment history, photographs, and other aspects of my life are private. They should not automatically be treated as matters for public disclosure merely because someone has obtained or possesses information about them.

In particular, some experiences in my life are personal and should not be publicly disclosed, circulated, discussed with unrelated persons, or used to construct a narrative about me without a legitimate and lawful basis.

The fact that an experience actually occurred does not, by itself, make it appropriate for public disclosure. Personal experiences should be treated with appropriate regard to privacy, context, relevance, confidentiality, and the circumstances under which the information was obtained.

Employment History and Background Information

Similarly, the fact that my employment history may be accurate, or otherwise positive does not, by itself, make disclosure of that history relevant, necessary, authorized, or appropriate in every circumstance.

Employment history and other background information should not be selectively disclosed merely to create an association, imply familiarity, establish a connection with another person, or encourage others to draw conclusions about me that are not supported by direct and reliable evidence.

Where employment history, personal experiences, or other background information is legitimately relevant to a particular matter, such information should nevertheless be presented accurately, in its proper context, and with appropriate regard to its source, purpose, relevance, completeness, and limitations.

Information that is irrelevant to the matter under consideration should not be introduced merely because it is available or because it can be used to construct a particular narrative.

Distinction Between Information and Personal Familiarity

The disclosure of information about me should not be used to imply that unrelated persons know me personally when they have never interacted with me.

A person's knowledge of facts or claims concerning me, whether obtained directly or indirectly, should not be represented as personal familiarity or direct knowledge unless such a relationship actually exists.

Any alleged connection between me and another person should be supported by specific, reliable, and objectively verifiable evidence.

Assumptions, coincidence, proximity, selective comparisons, indirect information, statements by persons without direct knowledge, or interpretations should not be presented as established facts.

Distinguishing Facts, Observations, Allegations, and Inferences

Facts, direct observations, information reported by others, allegations, assumptions, interpretations, inferences, and conclusions should remain clearly distinguished.

A person communicating information about me should, where appropriate, distinguish between what they personally observed or experienced and what they were told by someone else or inferred from circumstances.

Where information is incomplete, ambiguous, disputed, indirect, unverified, or capable of multiple reasonable interpretations, those limitations should be identified rather than concealed.

Uncertainty should not be converted into certainty merely through repetition or communication among multiple persons.

Repetition Does Not Establish Verification

The repetition of an allegation or assumption by several persons does not, by itself, transform that allegation or assumption into objectively verified fact.

Information should be evaluated according to its actual source, reliability, context, and evidentiary basis rather than merely according to the number of persons who have repeated it.

Context, Completeness, and Alternative Explanations

I also object to the selective presentation of information in which certain facts, photographs, communications, employment information, personal experiences, or observations are emphasized while the circumstances, context, alternative explanations, or absence of direct interaction are omitted.

Information should not be arranged or presented in a manner that creates a misleading impression of a relationship, intention, pattern, or course of conduct.

Legitimate and Lawful Fact-Finding

I do not object to legitimate and lawful fact-finding concerning genuinely relevant matters.

However, information concerning me should be obtained, evaluated, interpreted, used, retained, disclosed, and communicated accurately, fairly, lawfully, and with appropriate regard to its source, reliability, context, relevance, completeness, proportionality, and evidentiary significance.

Where information is obtained through observation, monitoring, investigation, records, other persons, or other means, the circumstances surrounding its acquisition should not be concealed when those circumstances are material to understanding its reliability or significance.

No Admission of Monitoring or Information-Gathering

Nothing in this statement should be interpreted as an admission that any particular person has monitored me, gathered information about me, communicated information about me, or constructed a narrative concerning me unless supported by specific evidence.

This statement records my objections to such conduct and establishes the standards by which I expect any information concerning me to be treated and represented.

Reservation of Rights and Objections

Nothing in this statement constitutes an admission or acceptance of any characterization, allegation, inference, or conclusion concerning me.

I expressly reserve all rights, objections, remedies, protections, and positions available to me under applicable law.
