PUBLIC DECLARATION, AND REQUEST FOR OFFICIAL CLARIFICATION

Concerning Personal Identity, Privacy, Safety, Personal Credibility, Professional Autonomy, Electoral Activities, Legal Documentation, Attribution of Statements, Financial Accountability, and the Right to Choose One’s Means of Livelihood

PRELIMINARY DECLARATION

I hereby issue this public declaration to establish my position and seek factual clarification concerning representations, communications, narratives, and personal characterizations that may be associated with my identity, private circumstances, professional interests, and possible involvement in political, electoral, or legal matters.

I consider it necessary to distinguish my actual personal experiences from fictional portrayals, statements originally made by other individuals, interpretations, assumptions, and assertions that have not been substantiated through reliable documentation.

Among the matters requiring clarification are recurring descriptions involving theater auditions, entertainment productions, film participation, shoe collecting, jewelry collecting, extravagance, pageantry, and other personal or occupational characteristics that I have not confirmed as factual descriptions of my actual life.

I also wish to document my concerns regarding communications involving lawyers, political personalities, and electoral activities, particularly circumstances in which statements or accusations initially introduced by other individuals may subsequently be presented as though they originated from me.

Additionally, I seek official clarification concerning any legal or electoral proceeding that may involve my identity, including the existence of complaints, petitions, summonses, subpoenas, hearing notices, administrative orders, or other documents requiring my attention.

The purpose of this declaration is to establish a reliable record, distinguish facts from interpretations, obtain appropriate information, protect my personal autonomy, and preserve any rights and remedies available under applicable law.

This declaration records my personal position and requests for clarification. It does not establish that surveillance, coordinated activity, harassment, intimidation, defamation, electoral misconduct, or any other unlawful conduct has occurred.

I. DECLARATION CONCERNING MY PERSONAL HISTORY AND ENTERTAINMENT ACTIVITIES

I hereby declare that throughout my life, I have never auditioned for any theater production, motion picture, television program, entertainment performance, or pageantry.

I have not established a personal or professional history of applying for film positions, seeking entertainment roles, participating in casting activities, or pursuing pageant opportunities.

Accordingly, any representation describing me as someone who has undertaken these activities should not be treated as an established description of my personal history without specific and reliable supporting evidence.

I request that my actual experiences be distinguished from fictional characters, imagined circumstances, assumptions, and narratives developed by other individuals.

My professional direction remains a matter of my own decision. I retain the ability to pursue employment, and other lawful means of earning a livelihood.

No entertainment-related characterization should be regarded as defining my professional identity or determining the direction of my occupation.

II. CLARIFICATION REGARDING THE MOTION PICTURE THE PLATFORM

I hereby confirm that I have watched the motion picture The Platform and I am familiar with its storyline and content.

III. REQUEST TO IDENTIFY THE ORIGIN AND FACTUAL BASIS OF RECURRING REPRESENTATIONS

I seek clarification regarding recurring descriptions that appear to associate my identity with particular interests, possessions, preferences, or occupational ambitions.

These include references to shoe collections, jewelry collections, extravagant lifestyles, pageantry, and entertainment activities.

I have not established these descriptions as factual representations of my personal history or circumstances.

I therefore request that anyone presenting these descriptions as factual information identify their original source, documentary basis, and intended context.

Where disclosure is legally permitted, I seek clarification concerning:

  • Whether the descriptions originate from actual personal information or fictional and interpretive material.

  • Who originally created or communicated the representations, where ascertainable.
  • Whether the material was subsequently modified, republished, or distributed by other individuals or organizations.
  • Whether personal information concerning me was used in developing or distributing the material.
  • What evidence supports any assertion that the descriptions reflect my actual circumstances.

I acknowledge that similar themes may appear in different forms of expression. Their recurrence alone does not establish coordination, surveillance, or improper intent.

Nevertheless, repetition should not replace factual verification when a representation is presented as a description of an identifiable person's actual life.

IV. CLARIFICATION REGARDING POSSESSIONS, FINANCIAL CIRCUMSTANCES, AND LIFESTYLE ASSUMPTIONS

I specifically request that descriptions associating me with shoe collecting, jewelry collecting, extravagance, luxury, or pageantry not be accepted as established facts without adequate evidence.

Statements concerning personal possessions, expenditures, financial resources, preferences, and lifestyle can create impressions that may not correspond to actual circumstances.

I therefore request that such descriptions be evaluated according to their source, reliability, and supporting documentation.

If these themes are intended as fictional illustrations, artistic expressions, or symbolic interpretations, they should be clearly distinguished from verified biographical information.

I further request that assumptions concerning my financial position, spending practices, lifestyle, and personal priorities not be treated as established facts without reliable supporting information.

VI. PRIVACY RIGHTS AND THE HANDLING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

I request clarification regarding whether information relating to my identity, communications, activities, personal preferences, appearance, private circumstances, or professional interests has been collected, processed, disclosed, or incorporated into materials associated with me.

Where applicable, I request information concerning:

  • The categories of personal information involved;

I request that my identity, experiences, statements, conduct, and professional history be represented in accordance with established facts.

Where fictional material or public commentary contains characteristics resembling my personal circumstances, appropriate distinctions should be maintained between creative expression and factual descriptions of my actual life.

I do not seek to prevent legitimate artistic expression, political discussion, or lawful commentary.
