Practical & Hands-On Support

Let's Make the Work Easier

Some projects need technical knowledge. Others need someone who can get involved, organise information, prepare documentation, coordinate tasks, follow up requirements or take care of work that is slowing everything else down.

My approach is practical and hands-on: understand what needs to be done, work through the details and help move the task toward a useful result.

My background covers software, electrical, electronics and hardware, construction-related environments, clinical and medical-related project work, research, documentation and technical project support.

Support can be arranged around the actual requirement — whether that means a single task, a defined project, temporary support or ongoing assistance.

You don't need to know exactly what service you need.

Tell me what you are trying to accomplish, what needs to be done and when you need it. We can identify the practical tasks involved and discuss the appropriate arrangement.

A hands-on approach means getting involved.

Where appropriate, I can work through the details with you rather than simply providing general advice.
